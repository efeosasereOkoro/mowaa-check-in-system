# Reception quick-start — Check-In / Check-Out

A 5-minute guide for the reception desk. Everything you need is on the **Dashboard**.

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the app link in the browser (Chrome recommended). No install needed.
2. Enter your **email** and **password** and tap **Sign in**.
3. You land on the **Dashboard** — this is your home screen all day.

> **Shared device?** When you hand the desk over, tap **Sign out** (top right) so the next
> person signs in as themselves. Every check-in / check-out is recorded against whoever is signed in.

---

## 2. Read the Dashboard

- The **day title** at the top shows which day you're viewing (e.g. *Day 1 — Tuesday, 4 August*).
  It opens on **today** automatically.
- The four tiles show the live count: **Registered · On-site · Checked out · Not arrived**.
- The **Roster** below lists every child with their status. Use the tabs (All / On-site /
  Checked out / Not arrived) or the search box to filter.

> Check-in / check-out only works on the **current day**. If you're viewing a past/future day
> you'll see a note and no check-in buttons — tap **Go to today** to get back.

---

## 3. Find a child

Under **Find a child** you have two ways:

- **Scan QR** — tap **Scan QR**, allow the camera, and point it at the QR code on the child's ID card.
  The child's card opens automatically. Works on any phone or laptop with a camera.
- **Search** — type the child's **name** or **tag number** (e.g. `TAG-001`) and pick them from the list.
  Always available as a fallback if a camera isn't handy or a card is missing.

The child's card opens with their name, age, tag, guardian name and phone, and current status.

---

## 4. Check a child IN

1. Find the child (step 3).
2. On their card, tap **Check in**.
3. The status flips to **Checked in** with the time. Done.

> One check-in per child per day. If they're already checked in, the button won't let you do it twice.

---

## 5. Check a child OUT

1. Find the child — they must be **Checked in**.
2. Tap **Check out**.
3. **Choose who is collecting** from the list of authorised pickup persons (guardian + anyone the
   admin added). This is required — it's recorded with the check-out.
4. Confirm. Status flips to **Checked out** with the time and the collector's name.

> **Someone not on the list?** If your desk is allowed to, you can add a pickup person on the spot
> (name + relationship) and then check the child out. If you're not sure, check with the admin.

---

## 6. What you can and can't see

As a receptionist you see what you need to run the desk: **name, tag, age, status, guardian name
and phone**. You will **not** see home addresses or any health/medical information — those are
restricted to other roles by design. That's normal, not a fault.

---

## Quick troubleshooting

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Check-in buttons are missing | You're not on **today's** view — tap **Go to today**. |
| "Already checked in / out today" | The child was already processed. An **admin** can override if it's a genuine exception. |
| Child not in search | Confirm spelling / tag number, or check with the admin — they may not be registered yet. |
| Handing over the desk | **Sign out** so the next person is recorded correctly. |
| Page looks stuck | Refresh the page; you stay signed in. |

That's the whole job: **sign in → find → check in / check out → sign out at handover.**
